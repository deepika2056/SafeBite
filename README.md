# SafeBite

AI-Powered Street Food Safety Platform

## Live Demo

https://safebite-connect.lovable.app/

## Problem Statement

Street food is affordable and widely consumed, but consumers often lack reliable information about hygiene standards. Vendors also receive limited constructive feedback for improvement.

## Solution

SafeBite Connect is an AI-powered platform that enables communities to discover hygienic street food stalls, submit hygiene reports, share reviews, and support vendors through actionable AI-generated insights.

## Key Features

- AI-powered hygiene review summaries
- Community hygiene reporting
- Tamil ↔ English translation
- Vendor dashboard
- Admin dashboard
- Hygiene score system
- Community-driven food safety awareness

## Social Impact

- Promotes safer food choices
- Encourages public participation
- Supports local street food vendors
- Improves food hygiene awareness
- Increases accessibility through multilingual support
